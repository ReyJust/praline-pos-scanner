## Look-alike pairs to watch in testing

The descriptions point at each other for these, but they're where mistakes will come from.

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| Tikal Chilli vs Dark Salted Caramel | Same dark cube. Corner flakes: red/orange chilli vs white salt |
| Dark Ganache 73% vs the dark cubes | Rectangle with brown cacao nibs vs cube |
| Mandarin & Yuzu vs Native Strawberry Gum Passion | Dark cube: soft pillow top vs sharp diagonal crease |
| Cafe Latte vs Native Macadamia Caramel | Milk cube: plain pillow top vs embossed lines |
| Milk Salted Caramel vs Native Macadamia Caramel | Salt flakes vs embossed lines |
| Hazelnut Crisp vs Zesty Lemon Tart | Milk block: big amber shards vs fine pale crumbs |
| Lime Cheesecake vs Zesty Lemon Tart | Thin milk bar: white stripes vs pale crumbs |
| Pina Colada vs Dark Davidson Plum | Coconut on one side vs red-purple powder on a corner |
| Native Green Ants vs Jam Donut Shot | Round: plain milk dome vs sugar-sprinkled |
| Mint Rush vs Caramelised Coconut | Same ridged oval: dark vs white |
| The three Sienna Strawberry hearts | Dark / milk / white |
| Baileys vs Golden Leatherwood Honey | Ribbed oval: plain dark vs gold-painted ribs |
